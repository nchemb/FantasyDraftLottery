## Sequence

1. **0:00 Cold open** — T1. Chyron slides in: "DRAFT NIGHT '96" + league name + "SEALED <date>" badge. Score starts.
2. **0:06 Stage reveal** — T2. Lower-third: "THE COMMISSIONER IS AT THE PODIUM". Empty retro draft board appears right side, all slots blank.
3. **0:12 Picks, bottom → top.** Per pick (~12s; quick mode ~7s when >16 teams):
   - T3 podium loop. VO (per-sale ElevenLabs): "With the tenth pick in The Gridiron Gang draft... the league selects: Last Place Larry."
   - Chyron wipe: team name slams into slot 10 on the board (VHS wipe + brass stinger).
   - Alternate T4/T5 crowd cut (2s).
4. **Final two** — pacing slows, drumroll, lower-third: "TWO REMAIN". Extra beat on T3 before the #2 call.
5. **#1 pick** — longer suspense on T3 → VO announces #1 → T6 confetti + name in gold at top of board.
6. **Outro** — full board holds, credits chyron: "Produced by Fantasy Draft Lottery · fantasydraftlottery.com", replay + share buttons.
