How to Set Your Fantasy Basketball or Hockey Draft Order

September 26, 2026 by FDL Team

Fantasy basketball and fantasy hockey leagues set their draft order the same two ways football leagues do: a random order where every team has an equal shot at the first pick, or a weighted lottery where the teams that finished worst last season get more lottery balls. Either one takes about a minute with a free draft order generator. Getting the whole league to trust the result is harder.

Random or weighted: which should your league use?

A new redraft league should go random, since nobody has a record yet. Keeper and dynasty leagues usually weight it, so a bad season buys better odds at the top pick. Better odds, though, not a guarantee. If last place automatically picks first, you've given someone a reason to bench their stars in week 12 (every keeper league has that owner).

How the real NHL draft lottery works

The NHL runs two lottery draws among the 16 teams that missed the playoffs. The worst team holds 185 of 1,000 combinations, an 18.5% chance at the first draw. A team that wins a draw can move up no more than 10 spots, which means the last-place team can fall no lower than third. [Elite Prospects]

To copy it in fantasy hockey, give the worst team the most balls and fewer to each team above it. Run the lottery for the top two or three picks only. Everyone else slots in by record.

How the real NBA draft lottery works, and the 2027 change

Since the 2019 draft, the NBA's three worst teams have each had a 14% chance at the first pick, with odds falling 1.5 points per spot after that and only the top four picks drawn. [NBA.com]

That changes with the 2027 draft. The league's Board of Governors approved a "3-2-1" system, 29 to 1, that draws all 16 lottery picks and gives the three worst records fewer balls than the other non-playoff teams. It's aimed squarely at tanking. [Yahoo Sports] If your dynasty league has a tanking problem, borrow it: give the bottom three a little less weight than the teams just above them.
