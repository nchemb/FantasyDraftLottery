What your league sees

At showtime, the link you shared turns into a broadcast. A retro arena on a rainy night. A commissioner at the podium. Then the announcer starts calling picks from the bottom of the order: "With the tenth pick in your league's draft... the league selects..." and the name slams onto the draft board. Pick by pick it climbs toward first overall, the room holds its breath between the last two names, and confetti falls on the winner.

The sample above runs about two and a half minutes with a six-team league. Yours uses your real team names, spoken out loud.

Nobody can accuse you of rigging it

The order is generated once, on our servers, the moment you pay. It gets locked with a timestamp and a cryptographic fingerprint before a single person sees it, and that includes you. There is no rerun button. When your league mate swears you kept rolling until you got the first pick, the seal says otherwise.

This is the part the free generator can't give you: with any client-side tool, a commissioner can quietly regenerate until they like the result. A sealed order can't be touched.

How it works

  1. Enter your league and team names on the home page, then hit the Draft Night button.
  2. Pick a broadcast time (or choose to start it yourself) and pay $9.
  3. You get two links: a share link for the league and a private commissioner link for you.
  4. At showtime everyone on the link watches the same broadcast at the same moment. Anyone who misses it gets the replay.

Works for 2 to 32 teams. Weighted odds carry over from the form, so your last-place friend can have his extra lottery balls.

    Questions leagues actually ask

    What if someone can't make it live?
    The same link plays the replay after the broadcast ends. They miss the moment, not the result.
    Can I change the time after I pay?
    Yes. Your commissioner link lets you move the broadcast time any time before it starts.
    Can I see the order early, as the commissioner?
    No, and that's the point. You find out with everyone else.
    Phones?
    Works on phones and laptops. Viewers tap once to join, which turns the sound on (phones require that tap before a page can play audio).
    What does it cost?
    $9, once, per league. The free generator on the home page stays free.
